# AIDE88 — Articles, Ideas, Discussions, Exchange

**A quiet place on the internet.**

AIDE88 is not another social network. It is a minimalist, text-only platform built for writers, thinkers, and readers who are tired of the noise of modern social media. 

We believe that the best way to connect is through words, not images or algorithms.

---

## 🏛️ Our Philosophy

The platform is built on four simple pillars:
*   **Articles**
*   **Ideas**
*   **Discussions**
*   **Exchange**

We believe in a **Clean Internet**. A space where you can read, write, and think without being chased by notifications, ads, or machines deciding what you should see.

---

## ✨ Features

*   **Text-Only Environment:** No images, no videos, no distractions. Just words on a page.
*   **No Algorithms:** The timeline is yours. You read what people actually wrote, in chronological order.
*   **The Daily Archive:** Every day that has words is recorded. You can go back and read what was shared on any specific day. Nothing gets buried by the "new."
*   **Exclusive Community:** We are strictly limited to **10,000 accounts**. A small, genuine community is better than a massive, noisy one.
*   **5 Posts Per Day:** To ensure quality over quantity, each user is limited to 5 posts per day.
*   **Flat Discussions:** Comments are simple, flat, and focused. No complex threads, no toxicity.
*   **No Private Chats:** We only communicate in public. No DMs, no private groups. 
*   **No External Notifications:** The site waits for you; it does not chase you. 
*   **5 Beautiful Themes:** Choose the reading experience that suits your eyes (Paper, Night, Sepia, Nord, Terminal).
*   **3 Languages:** Fully localized in English, French, and Arabic (with full RTL support).
